step2: dofiles
========================================================

ACTIVE PIPELINE (in order of execution):

1. code/IPMUS_CPS-ASEC_Prelim.do
     Data prep / merging / crosswalk.
     Reads:   raw CPS-ASEC .dta + Eloundou scores + CPI + BLS crosswalk + Census->SOC crosswalk
     Writes:  output/data/CPS-ASEC_analysis_Dec01.dta
              output/data/Eloundou_updated_crosswalk.dta
              output/data/FRED_CPI_cleaned.dta
     Purpose: Build the CPS-ASEC worker-year sample with merged beta_o exposure scores
              and CPI-deflated real wages.  Feeds everything downstream.

2. code/ASEC_Descriptives_3-30.do
     Descriptive statistics.
     Reads:   output/data/CPS-ASEC_analysis_Dec01.dta + raw/Eloundou/occ_level.csv
     Writes:  output/tables/descriptives/beta_summary.tex,
              output/tables/descriptives/top5_exposed.tex,
              output/tables/descriptives/bot5_exposed.tex,
              output/tables/descriptives/occ_group_exposure.tex,
              output/graphs/descriptives/kdensity_beta_overall.pdf,
              output/graphs/descriptives/kdensity_beta_education.pdf,
              output/graphs/descriptives/unemp_by_quartile_post2022.pdf,
              (additional unused kdensity / mean_beta / college_vs_exposure PDFs).
     Purpose: Produce the descriptive tables and kernel-density / quartile plots
              used in Section 4 (Data) of the draft.

3. code/Descriptives_educ_4-18.do  [supplementary descriptive, not currently referenced in draft]
     Reads:   output/data/CPS-ASEC_analysis_Dec01.dta + raw/Crosswalks/onet_task_statements.csv
              + output/data/Eloundou_updated_crosswalk.dta
     Writes:  output/graphs/descriptives/<task_count_vs_college>*.pdf (scatter plots)
     Purpose: Task-count vs. college-share scatter, by above/below-median exposure.
              Supporting material for the education heterogeneity narrative.

4. code/ASEC_Analysis_3-17(Het).do
     Main regressions + heterogeneity (experience, education) + within-occupation test.
     Reads:   output/data/CPS-ASEC_analysis_Dec01.dta + output/data/Eloundou_updated_crosswalk.dta
     Writes:  output/tables/unemployment_tableA.tex,
              output/tables/logwage_tableA.tex,
              output/tables/unemployment_tableB.tex,
              output/tables/logwage_tableB.tex,
              output/tables/unemp_tableA_col.tex,
              output/tables/unemp_tableA_noncol.tex,
              output/tables/logwage_tableA_col.tex,
              output/tables/logwage_tableA_noncol.tex,
              output/tables/het_within_occ_lnwage.tex,
              output/graphs/unemployment_stateYFE_plot.pdf,
              output/graphs/logwage_stateYFE_plot.pdf,
              output/graphs/unemp_stateYFE_plot_colComb.pdf,
              output/graphs/lw_stateYFE_plot_colComb.pdf,
              output/graphs/lw_within_plot.pdf,
              (many other intermediate plots per specification).
     Purpose: All main-text and appendix regression tables and coefficient plots
              used in Sections 6.1, 6.2, 6.3, 6.5 of the draft.

SUPPLEMENTARY (non-Stata):

5. code/model_graph.ipynb
     Python/Jupyter notebook.
     Writes:  output/model/fig_preai.png, output/model/fig_postai.png,
              and other model-illustration PNGs.
     Purpose: Numerical illustrations of the task-based toy model (pre-AI wage schedule,
              post-AI wage schedule under AI supply shock).  Standalone from the Stata pipeline.
